Reading: The Internet and the Web are not the sameLectura y comprensión

Many people use the words Internet and Web as if they meant the same thing, but they are different. The Internet is a global network of networks. Its rules, called protocols, let devices exchange data. TCP divides information into packets and checks that they arrive correctly. IP gives each packet the address of its destination.

The World Wide Web is a service that uses the Internet. Tim Berners-Lee proposed it in 1989 while he was working at CERN. Web pages are written in HTML and connected by links. When you type a URL, your browser asks a server for the page and then displays it. Email and online games also use the Internet, although they are not the Web.

Grammar: Talking about the futureGramática explicada

En inglés se usan varias formas según sea una predicción, un plan o un acuerdo ya fijado.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Willwill + verbo: predicción o decisión del momentoAI will change many jobs.
Negativawon't + verboThis website won't load.
Going toam/is/are going to + verbo: plan o evidenciaWe are going to create a blog.
Present continuousam/is/are + verbo-ing: cita fijadaI'm meeting the client on Friday.
Probabilidadmay / might + verboPrices might go down.
Condición de tiempowhen + presente, will + verboWhen the page loads, you will see the menu.
Fíjate en esto
  • Tras when y if, para hablar del futuro, se usa presente: «when it finishes», no «when it will finish».
  • Will no lleva to: «will change», no «will to change».
  • Adverbios útiles: probably, definitely, perhaps.

Writing: A blog post: predicting the futureModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Pon un título y una frase que enganche.
  2. Haz dos o tres predicciones, con su motivo.
  3. Termina con una pregunta para los lectores.

Modelo

Will we still need websites in 2040?
I think the Internet will look very different in the future. People will probably use voice assistants more often, so they might visit fewer websites. Videos and virtual spaces will become more common as connections get faster. However, I don't think personal blogs will disappear, because people will always want to share their ideas.
