Reading: Working in EuropeLectura y comprensión

Many IT workers consider a job in another European country. Citizens of European Union countries generally have the right to live and work in other member states. In addition, the Europass website offers free tools for creating a CV and describing skills, which can make applying abroad easier.

Before accepting an offer, look carefully at the contract. Check the salary, working hours, holiday and notice period. Salaries may look high, but the cost of living can also be higher. Language matters too. English is common in international IT teams, but learning the local language helps with daily life and shows respect for your colleagues. Rules are different in each country, so always check official information.

Grammar: Tense revisionGramática explicada

Elegir bien el tiempo depende de si hablas de algo habitual, actual, terminado, con conexión al presente o futuro.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Present simplehábito o hechoI work from home on Fridays.
Present continuousahoraI am writing a report.
Past simplepasado terminadoI finished the course in 2026.
Past continuousacción en cursoI was working when you called.
Present perfecthave/has + participio: experiencia o conexión con ahoraI have worked with Java.
Futurewill / going toI will send the file. / I'm going to apply.
Fíjate en esto
  • Con una fecha terminada se usa past simple: «I worked there in 2025», no «I have worked there in 2025».
  • Con for y since se usa present perfect si sigue siendo cierto: «I have lived here since 2020».
  • Participios útiles: do → done, write → written, work → worked.

Writing: A cover letterModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Di a qué puesto te presentas y dónde lo viste.
  2. Explica por qué encajas, con habilidades concretas.
  3. Agradece, ofrece disponibilidad y despídete formalmente.

Modelo

Dear Ms Smith,
I am writing to apply for the Junior Web Developer position advertised on your website.
I am currently studying web application development and I have experience with HTML, CSS and Java. I am hard-working and organised, and I enjoy working in a team.
I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application at an interview. Please find my CV attached.
Yours sincerely,
Alex García
