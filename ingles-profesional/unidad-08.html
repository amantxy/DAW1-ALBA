Reading: Small files, big decisionsLectura y comprensión

Multimedia files can take up a lot of space, so developers often use compression. Lossy formats, such as JPEG and MP3, remove some information to make files smaller. Lossless formats, such as PNG and FLAC, keep all the original data, but their files are usually bigger. For this reason, JPEG is often better for photos on a website, while PNG is better for logos with sharp edges.

Choosing an app involves similar decisions. A photo editor may offer more features than another one but be harder to learn. Before you install an app, look at recent reviews and check which permissions it requests. The most popular app isn't always the most suitable. The right choice is the one that does what you need without wasting space or exposing your data.

Grammar: Comparatives and superlativesGramática explicada

Se usan para comparar dos cosas o destacar una dentro de un grupo.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Corto, comparativoadjetivo + -er + thanThis file is smaller than that one.
Corto, superlativothe + adjetivo + -estIt's the fastest app.
Largo, comparativomore + adjetivo + thanThis editor is more powerful than the other.
Largo, superlativothe most + adjetivoIt's the most popular format.
Irregularesgood → better → the best; bad → worse → the worstThis version is better.
Igualdadas + adjetivo + asThis app is as fast as that one.
Fíjate en esto
  • No se dice «more fast»; se dice «faster».
  • Las palabras terminadas en consonante + y cambian a -ier: easy → easier.
  • Tras un comparativo se usa than, no that.

Writing: An app reviewModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Di qué app es y para qué sirve.
  2. Comenta sus mejores funciones y un problema.
  3. Compárala con otra y da una valoración.

Modelo

I've been using this photo editor for six months, and it's one of the best free apps I know. It's easier to use than many professional programs, and it supports JPEG and PNG. The filters are excellent. However, the free version has too many adverts, and exporting large files is quite slow. Overall, I'd give it four stars out of five.
