Reading: Waiters or machines?Lectura y comprensión

A quiet change is taking place in restaurants. Some places now let customers order from a tablet instead of waiting for a waiter. The tablet shows an e-menu with photos of every dish, so people choose faster and the kitchen makes fewer mistakes.

Supporters say the system is fun. While they wait, customers can play games, watch videos or check their social networks. Families can keep children busy, and owners sell more because people order drinks and desserts more easily.

However, not everybody is convinced. Some people think a tablet is no substitute for a friendly waiter. Tablets cost more than paper menus, and they must be cleaned and repaired. Above all, critics worry that technology is replacing human contact, and eating out is also a social activity.

Grammar: The passiveGramática explicada

La voz pasiva se usa cuando interesa la acción o quien la recibe, no quien la hace. Se forma con el verbo to be conjugado y el participio.

TiempoActivaPasiva
Present simpleTed writes emails.Emails are written by Ted.
Past simpleThe technician bought a new antenna.A new antenna was bought by the technician.
Present perfectThe IT teacher has repaired a computer.A computer has been repaired by the IT teacher.
Past perfectAnna had made a cake.A cake had been made by Anna.
FutureThe engineers will present the project.The project will be presented by the engineers.
Fíjate en esto
  • El agente (by…) solo se pone si es importante; a menudo se omite: «The system was installed in 2014».
  • Con dos objetos, el indirecto puede pasar a sujeto: «The students were given diplomas».
  • Usa el participio: was written, no was write.
  • La pasiva es frecuente en textos formales: «Malware is defined as…».
