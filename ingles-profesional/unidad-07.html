Reading: Avoiding risksLectura y comprensión

Security matters every time you send information online. Buying a train ticket or a book means giving your name, your address and often your credit card number. That data passes through several computers before it reaches the shop, and a hacker who breaks into one of them could steal it.

Ordinary users can reduce these risks. Malware such as viruses, trojans, worms and spyware can damage your computer, so do not download unknown programs and do not open email attachments from people you do not know. Keep your antivirus updated, because new threats appear every day.

Your browser can help too. Cookies are small files that websites store on your computer, and you can allow them, block them or delete them. Look for the padlock and the https address before you pay anything: a secure site uses an SSL certificate to prove who it is. Finally, use a strong password for each account and turn on two-factor authentication when it is available.

Grammar: ConditionalsGramática explicada

Los conditionals hablan de causas y consecuencias. Hay cuatro tipos, y los dos primeros son los que más usarás al principio.

TipoFormaEjemplo
Zeroif + present simple, present simple (algo que siempre pasa)If you enter a wrong password three times, the account is blocked.
Firstif + present simple, will + infinitivo (algo posible en el futuro)If you open that attachment, your computer will get infected.
Secondif + past simple, would + infinitivo (algo poco probable o imaginario)If I were the IT manager, I would install a firewall on every computer.
Thirdif + past perfect, would have + participio (algo que no ocurrió)If he had updated the software, the attack would have failed.
Fíjate en esto
  • En la parte con if no se usa will: «if you click», no «if you will click».
  • Unless significa if not: «Your data is not safe unless you make backups».
  • En el second conditional es habitual y correcto «If I were…» con todas las personas.
  • En el third conditional va would have + participio, no would had.

Práctica

Discusión: ¿es grave descargar contenido ilegal? Formad cuatro grupos (artistas, usuarios, tiendas y productores). Cada grupo prepara sus argumentos según su papel y después debatís todos juntos. Puedes ver el planteamiento en el libro, p. 67.

Writing: An email of apologyModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Da las gracias por avisar del problema.
  2. Pide disculpas y explica qué ha pasado.
  3. Di qué vas a hacer y ofrece una compensación si procede.
  4. Cierra con una frase de cortesía.

Modelo

Dear Customer,

Thank you for bringing this matter to our attention. We are very sorry to hear that your new computer was infected with a virus. We apologise for the inconvenience.

It seems that the virus came with a program installed before the computer left our shop. We will look into it right away and send a technician to check your PC. Please find enclosed a voucher as compensation. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Yours sincerely,
IT Customer Service

Se guarda solo. No se corrige automáticamente.
