Reading: The weakest linkLectura y comprensión

Security is not only a technical problem. Many attacks begin with a person who trusts the wrong message. In a phishing attack, a criminal sends an email that looks real and asks for a password or a bank number. If the victim clicks the link, the attacker may steal that information. Ransomware is another serious threat. It encrypts files and demands money to unlock them.

Simple habits reduce these risks. Use a different strong password for each service and turn on two-factor authentication. Install updates quickly, because they often fix vulnerabilities. Make regular backups and keep one copy away from your main system. If you receive a strange message, stop and check it before you click anything.

Grammar: Zero and first conditionalGramática explicada

El zero conditional expresa algo que siempre ocurre. El first conditional expresa un resultado probable en el futuro.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Zero conditionalif + presente, presenteIf you lose your key, you can't read encrypted files.
First conditionalif + presente, will + verboIf you click that link, you will download malware.
Con imperativoif + presente, imperativoIf you see a strange email, report it.
Unlessunless = if notYour data won't be safe unless you make backups.
Modal en el resultadoif + presente, may/might + verboIf the password is weak, a hacker might guess it.
Fíjate en esto
  • No se usa will en la parte con if: «if you click», no «if you will click».
  • La frase puede empezar por el resultado; entonces no hace falta coma: «You will be safer if you update».
  • Unless ya contiene la negación: «unless you update», no «unless you don't update».

Modelo

Staying safe online
Phishing emails are a common way to steal passwords. If you receive an unexpected message, don't click any links. Check the sender carefully. Use a different password for each account and turn on two-factor authentication. If you think your account is in danger, change your password immediately and tell the IT department.

Se guarda solo. No se corrige automáticamente.
