Reading: The basics of computer networkingLectura y comprensión

A network is a group of devices connected so that they can share data and resources. A local area network, or LAN, covers a small place such as a home, a school or an office. A wide area network, or WAN, connects networks over long distances. The Internet is the largest example.

Inside a LAN, a switch connects devices and sends data to the correct one. A router connects the LAN to other networks. A firewall checks the traffic and can block dangerous connections. Devices can use cables or Wi-Fi. Cables are usually more stable, but Wi-Fi gives users more freedom to move. In a client-server network, clients ask for services and servers provide them.

Grammar: Modal verbsGramática explicada

Los modales no llevan -s, van seguidos del verbo sin to y expresan obligación, consejo, permiso o probabilidad.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Obligaciónmust / have toYou must change your password.
Prohibiciónmustn'tYou mustn't share your password.
Sin obligacióndon't have toYou don't have to restart it.
Consejoshould / shouldn'tYou should update the driver.
Probabilidad altamust / can'tThe cable must be broken. / It can't be the router.
Posibilidadmay / might / couldIt might be a Wi-Fi problem.
Fíjate en esto
  • Mustn't significa «está prohibido»; don't have to significa «no hace falta». Es el error más típico.
  • Phrasal verb: verbo + partícula con un significado propio, como set up o log in.
  • Con pronombre, muchos phrasal verbs lo llevan en medio: «turn it off».

Practica

Juego de rol: llamas a soporte porque no tienes conexión. La otra persona hace preguntas y te da dos consejos con should y might.

Writing: Giving adviceModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Describe el problema en una frase.
  2. Da tres consejos con should, must o might.
  3. Cierra ofreciendo más ayuda.

Modelo

Dear Marta,
I understand that your Wi-Fi is very slow. First, you should restart the router. You should also move it to a central place in your home. You mustn't share your password with too many people, because each device uses bandwidth. If the problem continues, it might be your provider, so you should call them.
Let me know if you need more help.
Best wishes,
Alba
