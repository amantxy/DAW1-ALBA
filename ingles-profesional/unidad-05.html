Reading: Choosing the right toolLectura y comprensión

Office software helps people work with information. A word processor is the best choice for letters and reports. A spreadsheet organises numbers in cells and can calculate results automatically with formulas. A database stores large amounts of organised information in tables, and users can find what they need with queries.

Many companies pay for a licence to use proprietary programs. Others choose open-source software, such as LibreOffice. In open-source projects, the source code is available, so anyone can study and improve it. This kind of software is often free of charge, but companies may still need to pay for training or technical support. The best tool depends on the task, the budget and the users.

Grammar: QuestionsGramática explicada

La forma de la pregunta cambia si preguntas por el sujeto o por el objeto, y si es directa o indirecta.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Pregunta directaauxiliar + sujeto + verboDo you use spreadsheets?
Pregunta de objetopartícula + auxiliar + sujeto + verboWhat did you save?
Pregunta de sujetoWho/What + verbo, sin do/didWho saved the file?
IndirectaCould you tell me + partícula + sujeto + verboCould you tell me where the file is?
Indirecta sí/noDo you know + if + sujeto + verboDo you know if it is free?
Fíjate en esto
  • En preguntas de sujeto no se usa did: «Who wrote this?», no «Who did write this?».
  • En indirectas no hay inversión: «where the file is», no «where is the file».
  • Las preguntas indirectas suenan más educadas, sobre todo con clientes.

Practica

Juego de rol: un cliente se queja porque ha perdido una hoja de cálculo tras una actualización. Discúlpate, pregunta qué pasó y ofrece una solución.

Writing: A surveyModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Escribe una introducción con el objetivo.
  2. Prepara cinco preguntas directas y variadas.
  3. Agradece la participación.

Modelo

Office Software Survey
We want to know how students use office software. It only takes two minutes.
1. Which programs do you use most often?
2. How often do you use spreadsheets?
3. Who taught you to use them?
4. Do you prefer free or paid software? Why?
5. What would you like to learn next?
Thank you for your help!
