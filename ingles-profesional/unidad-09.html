Reading: We're hiring!Lectura y comprensión

TechNova is a growing software company based in Valencia. We create web and mobile applications for small businesses. We are looking for a Junior Web Developer to join our friendly team of twelve employees.

You will build and test websites, fix bugs and work closely with our designers. You must have a qualification in web development and a good knowledge of HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Experience with Java or databases is an advantage. You should also have an intermediate level of English. We offer a full-time contract, flexible working hours and training. To apply, send your CV and a short cover letter to jobs@technova.example before 30 April. This company and this advert are invented for practice.

Grammar: Reported speechGramática explicada

El estilo indirecto cuenta lo que dijo otra persona. Normalmente, el verbo retrocede un tiempo.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Presente → pasado«I work here.» →She said (that) she worked there.
Continuo«I am testing it.» →He said he was testing it.
Pasado → pasado perfecto«I finished.» →She said she had finished.
Will → would«I will call.» →They said they would call.
Can → could«I can start.» →He said he could start.
Tell / saytell + persona; say + sin personaShe told me... / She said...
Fíjate en esto
  • Tell necesita persona: «He told me», no «He told that». Say no: «He said that».
  • También cambian referencias: here → there, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day.
  • Pregunta indirecta: «Do you code?» → «She asked if I coded».

Practica

Prepara un vídeo CV de 1 minuto: quién eres, qué estudias, qué sabes hacer y qué buscas. No hace falta memorizarlo palabra por palabra.

Writing: A CVModelo y tu propio texto
  1. Datos de contacto y un perfil de dos líneas.
  2. Formación y experiencia, de lo más reciente a lo más antiguo.
  3. Habilidades técnicas, idiomas y proyectos.
