Reading: An accidental revolutionaryLectura y comprensión

In 1991, Linus Torvalds was a computer science student at the University of Helsinki. He wanted to understand his new computer better, so he started to write a small kernel as a personal project. He wasn't trying to change the software industry.

While he was working on it, he posted a message online and invited other people to give their opinions. Programmers around the world downloaded the code, found errors and sent improvements. Because the code was open, the project grew quickly. Today, Linux runs on most web servers, and it is also the base of Android. In 2005, Torvalds created another important tool, Git, to manage the development of Linux.

Grammar: Past simple and past continuousGramática explicada

El past simple cuenta hechos terminados. El past continuous describe una acción en curso en el pasado, a menudo interrumpida por otra.

FormaCómo se usaEjemplo
Past simple regularverbo + -edThe technician installed the driver.
Past simple irregularforma propia (write → wrote)She wrote the first version.
Negativadidn't + verboThe update didn't finish.
PreguntaDid + sujeto + verbo?Did you restart the computer?
Past continuouswas / were + verbo-ingHe was updating the system.
Con when / whilewhile + continuo; when + simpleWhile I was working, the screen went black.
Fíjate en esto
  • Tras didn't el verbo va en infinitivo: «didn't work», no «didn't worked».
  • Was va con I/he/she/it; were con you/we/they.
  • Irregulares útiles: go → went, make → made, find → found, see → saw, begin → began.

Modelo

Ada Lovelace was an English mathematician. She was born in London in 1815. As a young woman, she worked with Charles Babbage on his Analytical Engine, a machine that was never completed. In her notes, she described a method for calculating numbers with this machine. Today, many people consider it the first computer program. For this reason, she is an important figure in the history of computing.
